## Dynamic Art Signatures (动态艺术签名)

Names, objects and gradients share the letterform outline, using separate upper and lower strokes with a pause of about one second. The final stroke decelerates into a taper; unwritten areas have no ghost print, and there is no micro-shake or recoil. Levi and Aster are the iteration baselines; the other seven are expansion samples — technical checks are not per-design user acceptance.
