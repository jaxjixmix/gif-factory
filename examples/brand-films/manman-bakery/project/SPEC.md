# Clay characters × collage contraption: 15-second pilot

1920×1080 / 30fps / 15s. Reuses the three accepted original clay shopkeepers; borrows gbro-collage-broll's visual proposition, empty-scene assembly, few objects and first/last-state design. This version uses editable layered compositing and no Gemini video generation was invoked.

0–5s: the croissant reaches out to grip the paper strip and pulls, leaning back; the same shop, cut into three folds, unfolds in sequence and the text then lands in place. Action precedes result.
5–10s: the paper oven slots into place and its door swings open; the saucer-less bread arcs onto the one saucer held in hand; the saucer dips under the weight; the bread rises and the tray exits.
10–15s: the bread drops into the branded takeaway bag; toast and coffee peek out of the bag mouth, and the croissant greets customers beside the bag. The headline and the shop name on the bag hold steady for at least about 3 seconds.

Unified cream white/sage green/terracotta red. 3–6 object groups; no whole-frame fades or slow zooms standing in for assembly. Only deterministic translation, paper folding and occlusion per group. 16 character poses sharing one crop and scale. Every prop has one owner at a time; the bread layer carries no saucer. Real paper-bag front/back occlusion is kept.
