## What It Looks Like

Soft volumes, matte clay, bead eyes and consistent lighting.

## How It Moves

Continuous poses with tiny bounce-back, keeping the material and the character's personality intact.

Best for: fictional shops, brand IP, product sidekicks. Current reference size and pacing: 512×512, set by the action. These are the series defaults; when the user specifies a size or duration, follow the request.

## How to Produce

Reuse the approved character, art style and motion. Read [appearance and asset requirements](../../references/appearance.md#illustration) and [the production workflow](../../references/keyframes.md); don't carry over export defaults from another art style or use.

Clay visuals don't mean a 3D model exists; paper-collage scenes don't require turning the character into paper either.

Make a sample first for new characters and new motions; record technical checks and user approval separately. HD isn't full action, and a key-state switch isn't sustained performance.
