## What It Looks Like

Pixel figures with small bounces, sways and emoji.

## How It Moves

One expression and pose per GIF, small looping motion, fixed chat text.

Best for: sticker packs, small avatar animations. Current reference size and pacing: 320×320, usually about 2 seconds. These are the series defaults; when the user specifies a size or duration, follow the request.

## How to Produce

Reuse the approved character, art style and motion. Read [appearance and asset requirements](../../references/appearance.md#pixel) and [the production workflow](../../references/light-motion.md); don't carry over export defaults from another art style or use.

Generate real pixel clusters and stair-stepped outlines; don't pass off blurry downscaling as pixel art.

Make a sample first for new characters and new motions; record technical checks and user approval separately. HD isn't full action, and a key-state switch isn't sustained performance.

## Say It Like This

> Make 25 pixel light-motion GIFs, each with a different expression and pose, add a small bounce or sway, fixed text below.
