## What It Looks Like

Limited palette, ink lines, halftone dots, slanted-cut negative space and custom lettering, designed as one.

## How It Moves

The character switches from calm to entrance/burst; the whole key state changes together with the big type.

Best for: character promotion, event key visuals, show posters. Current reference size and pacing: 720×960, 6-second loop. These are the series defaults; when the user specifies a size or duration, follow the request.

## How to Produce

Reuse the approved character, art style and motion. Read [appearance and asset requirements](../../references/dynamic-posters.md) and [the production workflow](../../references/dynamic-posters.md); don't carry over export defaults from another art style or use.

Don't paste text onto an unmodified color image. The current nine are a lenticular switch between two complete posters, not independent glyph-by-glyph morphs.

Make a sample first for new characters and new motions; record technical checks and user approval separately. HD isn't full action, and a key-state switch isn't sustained performance.

## Say It Like This

> Make a P5-style comic-fusion GIF poster: character, background and text redrawn together, keeping the character's main color, hair and arm crossing in front of and behind the title, 6-second loop.
