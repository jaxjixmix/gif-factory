## What It Looks Like

Keeps the face, clothing and linework of the anime, chibi or existing illustration.

## How It Moves

Continuous key poses carry the character through a complete action, with hair and clothes following.

Best for: anime characters, character action stickers. Current reference size and pacing: 512×512, loop set by the action. These are the series defaults; when the user specifies a size or duration, follow the request.

## How to Produce

Reuse the approved character, art style and motion. Read [appearance and asset requirements](../../references/appearance.md#illustration) and [the production workflow](../../references/keyframes.md); don't carry over export defaults from another art style or use.

Check clothing structure and the continuity of face and hands. The Anya / Doraemon revised versions stay recorded under their own statuses and aren't automatically promoted to success templates.

Make a sample first for new characters and new motions; record technical checks and user approval separately. HD isn't full action, and a key-state switch isn't sustained performance.
