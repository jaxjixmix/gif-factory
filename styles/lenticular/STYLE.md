## What It Looks Like

The same composition shifts between two forms: person/mask, everyday/transformed.

## How It Moves

Two states hold while thin vertical strips sweep back and forth to switch; the card title usually stays fixed.

Best for: collectible cards, dual identities, product colorways. Current reference size and pacing: 480×720, 6-second loop. These are the series defaults; when the user specifies a size or duration, follow the request.

## How to Produce

Reuse the approved character, art style and motion. Read [appearance and asset requirements](../../references/lenticular.md) and [the production workflow](../../references/lenticular.md); don't carry over export defaults from another art style or use.

Check angle, eyeline, chin and shoulder line first; don't mask mismatches with black edges, a widened face or a stitched background.

Make a sample first for new characters and new motions; record technical checks and user approval separately. HD isn't full action, and a key-state switch isn't sustained performance.
